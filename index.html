Questions people ask

How much does a website cost?

A simple business website starts from ₹X,XXX and a Shopify store from ₹X,XXX. The price depends on pages or products, design and features. You get a fixed quote before any work begins.

How long does it take?

Most business websites take 1 to 3 weeks and online stores 2 to 4 weeks, depending on content and the number of products.

Which platform should I choose?

Shopify if you sell online every day, WordPress if you publish often, and a custom HTML site if you want something fast and low-maintenance. I'll recommend one honestly.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Shopify and WordPress sites come with a short training video made for your site, so you can change products, prices and offers yourself.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site is built with SEO basics: fast loading, proper titles, schema and Search Console setup. Local and brand searches usually appear within weeks.

Yes. Most work happens over WhatsApp and video calls, so I work with businesses across Tamil Nadu and India.
