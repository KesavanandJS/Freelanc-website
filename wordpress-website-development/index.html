Questions people ask

WordPress or Wix: which is better?

WordPress gives you full ownership, better SEO control and no lock-in. Wix is simpler to start but harder to grow and move away from.

Where will my site be hosted?

I recommend a reliable Indian or Singapore server for speed, set it up in your name and hand over every login.

Will I be able to add pages and posts?

Yes. You get a training video for your site showing how to add pages, posts, images and offers.

Do you look after updates?

Yes, through a monthly care plan that covers updates, backups, security checks and small edits.
