Questions Tiruppur business owners ask

Should my garment brand use Shopify or WooCommerce?

For most new Tirupur brands, Shopify is simpler: hosting, security and checkout are handled for you, and you can run it from your phone. WooCommerce suits businesses that already use WordPress or need deep customisation without monthly app fees.

Can my website handle sizes, colours and combo packs?

Yes. Every product can have size, colour and pack options with separate prices and stock. Combo offers, bulk discounts and buy-more-save-more pricing can be set up for families and resellers.

Do exporters really need a website if buyers come through agents?

Yes. Buyers and agents check you online before placing orders. A clear catalogue site with capacity, products and certifications builds trust faster than PDFs and helps new buyers find you directly on Google.

How long does it take to build?

Catalogue sites usually take 2 to 3 weeks and Shopify stores 3 to 5 weeks, depending on how many products you have and how quickly photos and details are ready. Your quote includes a clear timeline.

Will I be able to add new designs myself?

Yes. Adding a product takes a few minutes from your phone or computer. I provide a short training video made for your site, so your team can add new designs, change prices and run offers without help.
