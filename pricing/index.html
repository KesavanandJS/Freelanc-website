What affects the price

The number of pages or products, how custom the design is, the features you need (bookings, wholesale pricing, integrations) and whether you supply photos and text all shape the final price.

You'll always get a written quote with a fixed price and timeline before any work begins. If something new comes up mid-project, we agree the cost before I build it.

Questions people ask

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain and hosting are paid by you, in your name, so you always own them. I set everything up and tell you exactly what to buy.

How do payments work?

Usually 50% to start and 50% at launch, by UPI or bank transfer. Larger projects can be split into three stages.

Do you provide a GST invoice?

Invoice details depend on registration status; ask when you request your quote.

What if I need changes after launch?

You get 30 days of free fixes. After that, small edits are covered by a care plan or charged per change.

Not sure which package fits?

Tell me about your business and I'll recommend one, with a custom quote if you need something different.
