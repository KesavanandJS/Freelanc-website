Questions people ask

Can I edit an HTML website myself?

Text and images can be changed, but it needs a little technical comfort. Most clients send changes on WhatsApp and I update them, or pick a monthly care plan.

Is an HTML website good for SEO?

Yes. Hand-coded sites are fast and clean, which Google rewards. With proper titles, schema and content, they rank as well as any platform.

Do I pay monthly fees?

Only for hosting and your domain, usually a few thousand rupees a year in total. There is no platform subscription.

When should I choose WordPress instead?

If you plan to publish blog posts or change content every week, WordPress is easier to manage on your own.
