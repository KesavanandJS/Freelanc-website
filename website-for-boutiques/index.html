Questions people ask

Do I need a website if I sell on Instagram?

Instagram brings attention; a website turns it into orders. Customers can browse everything, pay instantly and come back without messaging you.

Can customers request custom stitching?

Yes. Products can include options for blouse stitching, measurements and notes at checkout.

Photography isn't included, but I can share a simple phone-photo guide so your products look consistent.
